About us

Oakline is a two-person shop. Dana mills and joins; Peter finishes and delivers. We have made furniture on Wealthy Street since 2014, from hardwood bought within 200 miles of the shop.

Every joint is mechanical — mortise, tenon, dovetail — so a piece can be taken apart and repaired instead of replaced. Send us a photo if something breaks, whenever that happens; repairs on our own work are free for the first ten years.
